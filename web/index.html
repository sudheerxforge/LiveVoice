LiveVoice is a voice AI companion for emotional support. Speak the way you normally would, happy, sad or somewhere in between, and it listens and answers back like a kind friend.

What it is

Just talk

No typing. Tap once and have a natural voice conversation, in real time.

Responds to how you feel

It acknowledges your mood first and adjusts its tone as your mood changes.

Your language, your voice

Over 100 languages and 30 voices, with an adjustable speaking speed.

Chats are saved

Pick up an old conversation where you left it. Every new session starts fresh and calm.

Please note: LiveVoice is an AI, not a therapist or doctor. It does not give medical or diagnostic advice. If you are in danger or thinking of harming yourself, contact your local emergency number or a crisis helpline.

Install on Android

  1. Tap Download for Android above and download LiveVoice.apk from the latest release.
  2. Open the file. If Android asks, allow Install unknown apps for your browser or file manager.
  3. Tap Install, then open LiveVoice.
  4. Allow microphone access when asked, so it can hear you.
  5. Tap to start talking.
This app is installed outside the Play Store, so Android may show a warning. It connects to a LiveVoice server to get a short-lived session token, so the server must be running for it to work.

How it works

1. Token

The app asks the LiveVoice server for a one-time token. The Gemini API key never reaches your phone.

2. Voice

Your voice streams directly to Google's Gemini Live API, so replies are fast.

3. Reply

Gemini answers in a natural voice that plays back on your phone.
