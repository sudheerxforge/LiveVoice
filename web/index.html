How it works

Fast, because it goes straight to Gemini

A small server hands the app a one-time token. Your voice then streams directly to Google, so replies arrive quickly and the real API key stays off your phone.

🔑

1. Token

The app asks the LiveVoice server for a short-lived, single-use token.

⚡

2. Stream

Your voice streams straight to the Gemini Live API.

🔊

3. Reply

Gemini answers in a natural voice, played back instantly.

Install

Get it on your Android phone

Open this page on your phone and follow these steps.

Download. Tap Download for Android and save LiveVoice.apk.

Allow installs. If Android asks, allow Install unknown apps for your browser or file manager.

Install. Open the downloaded file and tap Install.

Allow the microphone. LiveVoice needs it to hear you.

Start talking. Tap the button and say hello.

⚠️This app is installed outside the Play Store, so Android may show a warning. It needs the LiveVoice server to be running in order to connect.
🛟Please note: LiveVoice is an AI, not a therapist or doctor, and it does not give medical or diagnostic advice. If you are in danger or thinking of harming yourself, contact your local emergency number or a crisis helpline.
